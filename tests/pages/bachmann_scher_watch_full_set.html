    We can offer you this UNWORN BELL & ROSS BRV2 MILITARY GREEN, ref. BRV292-MKA-ST, in stainless steel on a green elastic textile strap with a stainless steel hook closure. 

    Comes as a set of:

    - original over box and box

    - warranty card - 12.2024

    - warranty & user guide 

    With its khaki-green dial the BRV2 is a 41mm divers watch with the principle less is more.

    Simple 3 hand layout with Super-LumiNova C3 luminous mass on the hands as well as on the indices and a the date disk in the same greeen colour as the rest of the dial.

    The bi-directional rotating bezel is made out of Aluminium and gives this BELL & ROSS nice vintage vibes.

    Visible through the exhibition caseback is the automatic caliber BR-CAL.302 with a power reserve of 38 hours.

    High wearing comfort with the green elastic textile strap and a perfect every day watch for ladies and gentlemen.

     
    Under international BELL & ROSS warranty till 12.2026!
