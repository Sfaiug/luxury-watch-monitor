    CARTIER Vintage SANTOS DUMONT LADY Reference 96062 18K Yellow Gold Bj 90ies Spider Dial Paris

    This Cartier Santos Dumont Lady, reference 96062, is crafted in 18K yellow gold and embodies the unmistakable sophistication that has defined Cartier for over a century. Its slim, graceful proportions make it a perfect companion for the wrist, offering understated luxury.

    This particular example is preserved in very good condition and features a beautiful original, charming aged, Paris dial, prized by collectors for its authenticity and historical charm. The dial’s clean design, paired with classic Roman numerals and elegant blued hands, reflects Cartier’s signature aesthetic of balance and clarity. Enhancing its allure is the original crown, set with a deep blue sapphire cabochon—an iconic Cartier detail that adds a subtle yet unmistakable touch of refinement. Also with the watch is a Cartier service pouch.

    The watch is fitted with a black lizard strap, complemented by its original Cartier 18K yellow gold deployant clasp. Inside, it houses the manual-winding caliber 21, a reliable and beautifully traditional movement that speaks to the purity of classic watchmaking.

    The Santos Dumont is one of the most historically significant wristwatch designs ever created. Its origins date back to 1904, when Louis Cartier designed a watch for the pioneering aviator Alberto Santos-Dumont. At a time when pocket watches were the norm, Santos-Dumont required a practical solution that would allow him to check the time while flying. Cartier responded with one of the first purpose-built wristwatches, marking a pivotal moment in the evolution of modern watchmaking.

    The square case, exposed screws, and clean geometry of the Santos became instantly recognizable and have remained largely unchanged for over a century. The Santos Dumont line represents a more refined and minimalist interpretation of the original, emphasizing elegance, slimness, and dress-watch sensibility.
